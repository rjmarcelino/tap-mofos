Editing
Shown at the top of your page and fades out before the first button. Leave empty to keep the current photo.
Drag on the photo to choose the area to focus on, and use the slider to zoom. The white box shows what stays visible. The live preview shows the result.
A "Save contact" button appears on the page when it has a phone number or an email link (mailto:you@example.com).

Settings

These are stored only in this browser. They tell the builder which GitHub repo to save your pages to. The token needs access to this repo only, with Contents set to Read and write. Used for the saved link, QR codes, and share previews. Leave empty to use your github.io address.
